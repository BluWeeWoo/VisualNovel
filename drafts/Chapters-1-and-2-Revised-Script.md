**For review only. These revisions have not been implemented in the game.**

This document combines the current authored Chapters 1 and 2 with the proposed carpentry setup and the new Chapter 2 continuation. Existing dialogue is preserved except in the marked revised scenes. New dialogue follows the conversational draft approved in chat; watching TV replaces the discarded book-reading option.

### How to read

MC is the player character; [MC name] is their chosen name. Unlabelled text is narration. Conditional passages are alternatives, not consecutive dialogue. Scene links show where each choice continues. Shared scenes appear once. The porch’s repeated topic menus are combined into one hub without dropping their dialogue. Historical nightmare outcome branches remain labelled for reference. The free-form chat feature has no fixed script and is not invented here.

### Proposed continuity

- Rowan wants a paid carpentry apprenticeship with Mang Nestor. His father, Mayor Sanchez, expects him to learn municipal work and eventually run for office.
- MC knew Rowan’s father lived elsewhere during childhood, but does not know he is now mayor. His election occurred during their years apart. This is proposed background, not a claim about previously established canon.
- Mayumi supports Rowan making his own decision and challenges his avoidance.
- Rowan’s care remains sincere; his wish that MC stay longer is a personal hope, never an obligation or forced romance.
- The waiting activities are solo: a separate gardening minigame, channel-surfing, or resting on the porch. A couple of hours pass in narration before Rowan returns. The TV changes to the news only after his arrival. Watching TV is optional. Only that branch receives the later television callback. Visiting Lola alone remains a valid choice.
- The carpenter conversation in Chapter 1 remains optional. Later scenes supply the necessary information for players who skipped it.
- Lunch happens after the existing waking sequence. The current short Chapter 2 ending becomes a bridge into lunch; the new ending follows Mayumi’s visit.

### 01 / The road back

*Scene: journey*

It’s been eight hours since we left the city, including the stopover. I can’t get comfortable anymore.

I move my knee away from the seat in front of me. The window keeps rattling beside my head.

I grew up in Saint Luis with Lola nearby.

My parents took me to Manila when I was eleven.

I kept hoping I’d come back the next year. I never did.

Twelve years have passed. I’m twenty-three now.

The sea appears between two buildings. I lean closer to the window to get a better look.

**MC (thought):** I’m finally back in Saint Luis.

There are small fishing boats in the bay. I can see the old baywalk along the shore.

Lola used to take Rowan and me for walks there in the afternoons.

She held our hands when we crossed the road. Me on one side, Rowan on the other.

He’d complain that we weren’t little anymore. Then keep holding on after we’d crossed.

**MC:** Hay. I wonder how he’s been.

Thinking about him makes me smile.

Then I remember Lola’s funeral.

**MC (thought):** He was probably at the funeral.

Two years ago, Lola died during finals week.

My parents said I couldn’t miss my exams.

I wanted to argue. Instead, I asked if we could go afterward.

“We’ll talk about it.”

We never did.

I still took the exams. Handed everything in on time.

**MC (thought):** I was her grandchild, and I wasn’t there.

This trip is different.

I paid for the ticket with money I’d saved.

I told my parents I was leaving. I didn’t ask.

Then I left before we could finish another argument.

I needed a break from college. From home. From having the same conversation in my head.

Lola’s house needs cleaning. A few things probably need fixing.

**MC (thought):** I can do that.

**MC (thought):** Sweep a floor. Fix a window. Get some sleep.

**MC (thought):** That was the plan.

I look back toward the baywalk.

**MC (thought):** What am I going to say if I see Rowan?

**MC (thought):** Sorry I stopped calling?

**MC (thought):** Sorry I believed you’d forgotten me?

**Conductor:** SAINT LUIS! SAINT LUIS!

The shout makes me jump. It wakes the passenger across the aisle too.

**MC:** Grabe.

**MC (thought):** At least I’m not the only one he scared.
