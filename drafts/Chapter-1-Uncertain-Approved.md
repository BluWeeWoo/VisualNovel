# Chapter 1 — approved Uncertain revision

Scene headings identify the game passages. MC uses the player's chosen name; italics are thoughts. Shared travel incidents, family context in the opening, morning messages, and the chapter ending remain unchanged. The envelope discovery and letter flashbacks are skipped on this route.

## journeyStart
It’s been eight hours since we left the city, including the stopover. I can’t get comfortable anymore.
I move my knee away from the seat in front of me. The bus rattles so much that I can’t even rest my head against the window.
*Guess some things never change.*
*Malubak pa rin ang daan dito. Haha.*
I grew up in Saint Luis with Lola nearby.
My parents took me to Manila when I was eleven. They hoped we’d have a better life there—better schools, better opportunities.
It didn’t turn out the way I’d imagined.
I kept hoping I’d come back to Saint Luis at least once a year. I never did.
Twelve years have passed. I’m twenty-three now.
Everything feels familiar, but different at the same time.
The sea appears between two houses. I lean closer to the window to get a better look.
*I’m finally back in Saint Luis.*
Small fishing boats sit in the bay. Beyond them, I spot the old baywalk.
*The baywalk…*
Lola used to take me there in the afternoons. She always made sure we headed back before it got dark.
*I wonder what the house looks like now.*
I try to picture the rooms. Her chair by the window. The drawers that never closed properly.
There will be things to sort through. I don’t know how much has been left untouched.
*One room at a time. I can start there.*

## journeyEnd
I check the pocket where I put the house key.
*Okay. Get settled. Put my things down. See what needs doing.*
*That’s a plan.*
*Kaya ’yan.*
My parents will want another conversation eventually. Right now, I need a little room to decide what I want to say.
I turn back to the window and watch for my stop.
