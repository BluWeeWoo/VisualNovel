ABOUT THIS FILE
This is the script currently used by new games, from the bus ride through Rowan recognizing the MC. It includes every choice branch. The older chapter draft is not part of this opening.

You can edit the dialogue and add new scenes directly in this file. Changes here do not automatically change the game; send the edited file back when you want them implemented.

READING NOTES
MC is the player character, age 23. Rowan is 23 and uses he/him.
{name} means the player’s chosen name. Keep this placeholder where needed.
NARRATION is the MC’s internal narration.
Directions in brackets describe the current game presentation.
SCENE IDs and GO TO directions show how choices reconnect. Alternative branches do not all play in one run.
Artwork stays on screen between lines unless a direction changes it. Backgrounds and CGs transition smoothly; reduced-motion settings are respected.
No Rowan sprite appears before his recognition scene.
