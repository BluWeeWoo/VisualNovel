Rowan & Mayumi: consistent artwork

The main issue is a combination of drawing style and sprite scale. Mayumi has softer, more realistic rendering, while Rowan has stronger drawn outlines. The game also displays Rowan at roughly 1.55 times the generic sprite height used for Mayumi, making her appear farther away.

287 files reviewed · 36 referenced files proposed for correction · 2 new artwork previews
The inventory includes 284 raster files and three legacy SVG placeholders. Duplicate formats, reference sheets, unused drafts and unrelated assets are included in the audit, not counted as artwork needing replacement.

These are approval previews. Original assets, story, dialogue, choices, relationship values, saves and game code remain unchanged. The staging comparison is a separate mockup, not an implemented game screenshot.

1. Conversation scale and style

Keep Rowan’s approved sprite. Bring Mayumi to the same apparent conversational depth while preserving a natural height difference. Normalize head scale and body anchors rather than making both characters the same height.

The mockup uses a neutral expression to demonstrate the design. The final expression for “Did you answer?” should be concerned. Its simplified dialogue panel is only for context; no interface redesign is proposed.

2. Mayumi sprite standard

Preserve her mature face, brown eyes, low bun, jewelry, embroidered cream blouse, sage skirt and sandals. Use clearer linework and restrained shading that sits naturally beside Rowan. Apply the approved result to all seven expressions and preserve their different poses.

  • Mayumi’s seven sprites: unify face, hair and fabric rendering. Keep her age and individuality; she should not become a female version of Rowan.
  • Mayumi CGs: match the family dinner, birthday, father reveal and porch artwork. Correct the blue floral skirt patch in the father-reveal CG to her established sage skirt; leave Lola and Enrich untouched.

  • Rowan: retain the ten current upgraded sprites as the adult standard. Selected CGs need small face, eye or linework corrections. Preserve the age-eleven set, intentional chibi proportions and alternate gallery outfits.
  • Lighting: check nighttime sprite grading against each background. Preserve warm light on hair and skin; avoid applying a second dark filter to artwork already painted for night.
  • Gallery and previews: some gallery pictures use separate cropped reference sheets. Track those alongside live assets so an old crop does not remain after an approved replacement.
  • Historical assets: keep old drafts, shorter-leg versions, alternate garden outfits, source sheets and placeholders intact. Do not reactivate them.
