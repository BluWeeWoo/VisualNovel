# Proposed character consistency standard — awaiting approval

Identity anchors: assets/references/rowan-original.png; assets/portraits/rowan-upgraded/neutral.png and the matching expression set; assets/portraits/mayumi-v2/neutral.png for Mayumi’s identity and outfit. The new Mayumi preview is a proposed rendering correction, not yet an approved replacement.

- Match the approved adult Rowan’s fine warm-brown drawn outlines and controlled soft shading. Avoid mixing his drawn face with photorealistic skin or glossy 3D fabric on Mayumi.
- Rowan: preserve blue eyes, ash-brown hair, long bangs and low bun at age 23, earrings, lean adult proportions, white shirt, gray dog/bone cardigan, dark trousers and sneakers. Preserve scene-specific gardening shirt and hat. Do not widen the approved soft smile.
- Mayumi: preserve her distinct mature face, warm brown eyes, warmer complexion, brown low bun, cream blouse with olive embroidery and lace, sage tiered skirt, gold jewelry and sandals. She remains a mother in her forties, not a younger female copy of Rowan.
- Eleven-year-old Rowan keeps short hair, child proportions, ordinary shirt, shorts and slippers. Eighteen-year-old Rowan keeps short school hair and white uniform. Neither gets the adult bun or cardigan unless the scene explicitly calls for it.
- Compare face scale and visible body anatomy, not only PNG canvas size. Both characters in a conversation must read as occupying the same camera plane. Keep a plausible height difference without making Mayumi look farther away. Proposed screenshot scaling is illustrative and must be checked on desktop before implementation.
- Keep environmental light direction, warm/cool balance and cast shadows. Do not neutralize intentional golden sunlight or nighttime warmth. Avoid applying a heavy night filter twice to already-lit artwork.
- Preserve camera, background, props and acting. Only move a figure when perspective requires it. Keep Mayumi at the porch door; keep the dinner seating. Preserve Lola’s corrected hands, Enrich and Nestor unchanged.
- Keep all source assets. Approved replacements go to new versioned paths; switch consumers together (story, gallery crops, phone/profile images and save previews) while keeping gallery keys and save data stable.
- New art must be compared against the same identity anchors, the corrected approved master if accepted, and adjacent scenes. Check expression, age, outfit, head scale, hands, contact with props, and time of day before export.

Generated previews use the built-in image-generation tool. No replacement is installed by this review.
